### Sol B-634-2 (unfinished reminders stranded once the session leaves the due band): CLOSED
- **Recovery is independent of the due band.** `collectRecoverableWork` (`src/scheduling/jobs/reminder.job.ts`) reads the kind's unfinished rows directly: `retry`, or `sending` with an expired or null lease. It reads them oldest first, bounded at 200 per sweep.
- **What each row needs before it is re-sent:**
  - the session still confirmed;
  - the same start the row was claimed for;
  - the start still ahead of the cutoff (0 for 1 h; 65 min for 24 h, so a "tomorrow" reminder is never sent once the 1 h band is reached);
  - the recipient still a participant;
  - attempts left.
- **Bounded re-send.** A recovered session re-claims only the recipients with unfinished rows (`onlyUsers`). It reuses the existing lease/token takeover, which keeps per-channel receipts (in-app is not duplicated, push is re-sent). Sessions back in the band are left to the band pass, so nothing is claimed twice.
- **Retirement.** Rows that can never be sent are closed as `gave_up` with `last_error = retired:<reason>`, through a compare-and-set on `(status, attempts, claim_token)`, so a concurrent owner keeps the row. Receipts are untouched.
- **Fence.** The delivery fence now also refuses a session that has started. A taken-over row is closed (`retireClaim`) rather than deleted, so its receipts survive.
- **Tests:** the new `S-SCHED-4 B-634-2` block in `test/scheduling-lifecycle-integrity.spec.ts` advances the clock by the real cron interval. It covers:
