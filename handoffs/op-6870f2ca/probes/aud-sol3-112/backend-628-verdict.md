### B-628-6 — PARTIALLY CLOSED: durable notice rows are not claimed before delivery

`src/checkout/dunning-v2/dunning-v2.service.ts:363-392,401-430,444-489`: retry workers read the same pending/failed rows, call the transports, then unconditionally store receipts and `attempts = staleSnapshot + 1`; there is no per-delivery CAS/lease before the side effect. An independent barrier-controlled probe ran two `retryDueNotices` workers through the actual dispatcher on one due failed Day-1 client push: **two push sends**, and both write the same attempt number. This is not a hypothetical scheduler claim collision: the outbox retry itself has no ownership fence. Durable rows and fresh cycle keys close the previous persistence/key subcases, not concurrent delivery or stale receipt overwrite. [Candidate and claimed closure](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/pull/628), [prior finding](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/pull/628#issuecomment-5946585040).

**Minimal fix:** atomically claim each eligible, due delivery before calling its transport; use a fenced attempt owner/expiry and conditional receipts, preserving live-cycle cancellation. Provide transport-level stable idempotency/deduplication where supported rather than treating a lease as proof against an already-sent/lost-reply duplicate. Add concurrent-worker, stale-receipt, crash/lost-ack and cycle-ended-during-retry tests; do not dispatch unrelated not-yet-due rows merely because another channel in the group is due.

### B-628-8 — PARTIALLY CLOSED: one won charge clears unrelated outstanding disputes

`src/checkout/dunning-v2/dunning-v2.service.ts:860-924`: `onDisputeClosed` resolves a purchase from the winning charge, then `resolveDisputeCycle` unconditionally resolves that purchase's entire reversal cycle and restores entitlement without checking the other disputed charges. Independent probe: two charges belonging to one purchase, one dispute `won`, another `needs_response`, and an active locked reversal cycle; processing the won event returns `resolved: true`, sets the cycle to `resolved` and entitlement to `true`, while the other dispute remains open. The added single-dispute Day-10/renewal/card-update tests pass, but do not close the original obligation-identity problem. [Candidate implementation](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/pull/628), [prior finding](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/pull/628#issuecomment-5946585040).

**Minimal fix:** persist/match the charge/dispute obligation identity and transactionally aggregate outstanding obligations before lifting a purchase-wide reversal lock; an old or different won event must not clear an unrelated open/lost obligation. Cover multiple charges, out-of-order/replayed closures and simultaneous dispute-created/dispute-closed transitions. This does not challenge the agreed lost-dispute support path or require a new repayment policy.

### B-628-11 — NEW: per-invoice money intent is journaled after the Stripe side effect

`src/checkout/client-billing.service.ts:603-612,1215-1257`: the operation can exist durably with `lines: []`, but its invoice identity/result is saved only **after** pay/void. Injecting the first post-money journal-transaction failure exposes a recovery hole: Stripe's stateful test double collected **15,000 USD cents once**, the first response was `payment_uncertain`, and retrying the **same SetupIntent** returned `saved` with `amount_paid_cents: 0`, because the now-paid invoice is absent from `listOpenInvoices` and absent from the persisted operation. The corresponding cancel probe voided a 15,000-cent invoice, failed that journal write, then resumed as `ended` with `voided_invoice_count: 0` and `voided_amount_cents: 0`. Thus the new reconciler cannot reconstruct receipts for a side effect whose invoice identity was never committed; Stripe invoice idempotency alone does not preserve the application's money truth. [Candidate implementation](https://github.com/BradleyGleavePortfolio/growth-project-backend/pull/628).

**Minimal fix:** commit each planned invoice action, canonical identity, currency/approved amount, phase and idempotency key **before** calling Stripe. Recover in-flight actions from canonical Stripe state and persist their terminal receipts before declaring an operation complete. Add crash/DB-failure probes before and after every pay/void/journal boundary, including same-intent recovery and background reconciliation; preserve known collected/forgiven amounts on every replay. Do not remove multi-plan functionality.
