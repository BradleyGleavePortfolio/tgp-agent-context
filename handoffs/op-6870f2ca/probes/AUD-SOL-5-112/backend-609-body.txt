## What
C05 items 6 and 7 from the clinic onboarding contract. Full design: [docs/clinic-engagement.md](docs/clinic-engagement.md).

1. **Coach welcome message** (`src/engagement/coach-welcome.service.ts`)
   - 1-minute `@Cron`, same claim-by-write pattern as `DripDispatcherCron`.
   - Sends exactly one message from the client's coach into their existing thread at onboarding `completed_at + 13 min`, through `MessagingService.sendAsCoach` (same thread row, realtime ping, in-app notification, audit, analytics and PTM signals as a coach's own message).
   - `CoachWelcomeMessageJob.client_id @unique`: one job per client, ever.
   - Per-coach setting (default off) and template with `{first_name}` and `{coach_first_name}`. The coach's welcome text is runtime data set at C04 and never enters the repository; code ships only a generic default.
   - Set by the owner-only `GET/PUT /api/admin/coaches/:coachId/welcome-message` or `scripts/set-coach-welcome-message.ts --coach-email ... --template-file <private path> --enable` (never prints the text).
2. **Workout reminders** (`src/engagement/workout-reminder.service.ts`, `workout-reminder.policy.ts`)
   - 5-minute `@Cron` (fixed cadence).
   - Pushes at the client's S2 time (morning 07:00, midday 11:30, evening 17:00, varies or unanswered 09:00), in their local timezone from `NotificationPreferences.timezone`, on C1 and on every plan day.
   - At most one per local day (the `(client_id, local_date)` unique claim is taken before the send); skipped when that day's session is logged; skipped during the Day-10 dunning lockout (FEATURE_DUNNING_V2 on).
   - Opt-out through `workout_reminder_push` / `workout_reminder_inapp` (default true). The mobile toggle is mobile #312.
3. Migration `20270213000000_clinic_engagement`: additive, with RLS and a `down.sql`.

## Notes / open risks
- Push for coach messages: `MessageReceivedEmitter` writes `channel: 'push'` notification rows, but no code sends those rows to Expo (only `pushToUser` call sites send). The welcome message behaves exactly like a normal coach message, so it reaches the thread, the realtime ping and the in-app inbox, but not the lock screen, until coach-message push delivery is fixed in messaging (cross-cutting; not patched here to avoid a double push later). Workout reminders call `pushToUser` directly.
- Erasure inside the deletion transaction: backend #608's erasure manifest should also list `CoachWelcomeMessageJob` (client_id, coach_id), `CoachWelcomeMessageSetting` (coach_id) and `WorkoutReminderDelivery` (client_id), whichever of #608 / #609 merges second. The per-tick sweep here is the backstop either way.
- Turn a coach's welcome setting on before the first client completes onboarding; clients who completed earlier are deliberately not welcomed.
